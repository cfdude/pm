## Verdict per writer
- Every hit in the first list lives in a verb the effects registry marks effect:mutates and not hook (update-epic, remove-epic, sync, upgrade, pop-detour/push-detour, add-epic, add-many, set-spend-guard, ...). They all run inside main(), so the dispatch wrapper's before/after comparison in conductor.mjs covers them WITHOUT being listed (design D1): the wrapper compares the on-disk block with guardConfigured(after) after any verb whose save moved state.revision.
- Hook paths that write state: commit-nudge and render's reconcileArchived() self-heal (onConflict:"skip"). Excluded by design D1a: the wrapper takes no before-image for a hook verb, so a hook adds no read and rewrites no CLAUDE.md. A liveness change a hook's heal made is picked up by the next non-hook write, because the comparison is against the block ON DISK, not only against this invocation's before-image (covered by the hook test).
- set-spend-guard keeps its own writeRules(); the wrapper finds the block already agreeing and does not rewrite (D3), asserted by the one-refresh-line test.
- Inverses: archive <-> un-archive and add <-> remove are covered by the same comparison (asserted both ways).
