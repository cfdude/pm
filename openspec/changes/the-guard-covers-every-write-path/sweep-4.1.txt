WHERE THE RULE DOES NOT HOLD, and why
  - `find … -exec sed -i …` / `xargs … sed -i`: not a position the command-word arm reads.
  - every undecidable form: a variable-built path, `eval`, a script by name, `python -c`/`node -e`,
    a program that writes of its own accord. Named in the spec, the message and the docs.
  - an unreadable record allows every Bash call carrying a command (D4); an absent record leaves
    the guard dormant (the plugin's standing contract, and why destroying the record is a shape).
