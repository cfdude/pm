## Conclusions
- Consumers of the report: `spend-command.mjs` (spend), `spend-guard-verbs.mjs` (spend-guard) and now `spend-analysis.mjs`.
  (`activity-report.mjs` and `overhead.mjs` define unrelated functions of the same name.) The analysis calls
  `buildReport` and `attribute` exactly as `spend` does and adds no transcript reader: its only ledger access is
  `ingestForReport` (shared with spend, spend-guard and dev-log, so developer-log capture stays decided in one place) and
  `readLedger`. With tracking off neither is called.
- The tier mapping `tier-mismatch` reads is the ledger's: `tierOf()` (spend-reader.mjs:100) writes `row.tier` from
  KNOWN_MODELS; `buildReport` picks an agent's majority tier; the rule reads that value and defines no second mapping. Its
  own lists (TIER_MISMATCH) name tiers that are KNOWN_MODELS members.
- Inverse: the verb is read-only and writes nothing to state.json. The ingest it performs has `spend --clear` /
  `spend --rebuild` as inverses already; the developer log's `dev-log --clear` is unaffected. No new record is created, so
  no inverse is owed.
- Where the rules hold: low-coverage and invalid-markers are ledger-wide by design (an unattributed row has no epic);
  the other four honour --epic / --release. The trend is ledger-wide. Stated in commands/spend-analysis.md.
