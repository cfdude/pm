── A NOTE ON THE ARRAY'S ORDER, recorded because it is a property of the ENGINE and not of this change ──

`attributedCommits` is APPEND-ONLY and the engine neither reorders nor de-duplicates it (required
task item 4). This change attributed its work forward, and then had to CATCH UP four commits that
landed EARLIER — three proposal commits from before the first attributed one, and 2.1/2.2 re-attributed after its commit was
amended. Appending them put an ancestor of an already-attributed commit at the END of the array,
which breaks the invariant the array is read with (every attributed commit must be REACHED by the
recorded Gate 2 headSha, which is the LAST attributed commit). The repair is to re-attribute the
true head, e6b928f, so the last entry reaches every other one — the duplicate is what makes it
hold, and that is a shape the engine tolerates rather than intends. Filed as feedback (5.6).
