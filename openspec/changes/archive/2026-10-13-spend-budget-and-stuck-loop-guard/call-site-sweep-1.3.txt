== 3. REMOVE paths for this data (the data-reference sweep)
   `gateRounds` holds no other record's id, so no dangling pointer is possible.
   remove-epic deletes the epic object, and the tally goes with it (it lives ON the epic).
   The archive keeps the epic, and the tally stays as a harmless record. Neither needs to clear it (design D1:
   "a record of events"). `--withdraw-gate-review` is covered above.

== 4. Inverse of the operation (record-gate-review's tally)
   None shipped, justified in design D8: it is a record of events. A pass ends the streak; the operational
   "continue" is override-spend-guard. A reset verb would erase the evidence the guard reads.

== 7. OMISSIONS: paths where "a limit is configured" can FLIP without set-spend-guard (justified, not shipped)
   The rules block's `## Spend guard` section follows guardConfigured(state). set-spend-guard rewrites CLAUDE.md
   on the transition. Two other writes can also flip it:
   - `update-epic <id> --status archived` on the ONLY epic carrying an epic-layer limit (an archived epic's layer
     does not count);
   - `remove-epic <id>` on the same epic.
   Neither refreshes CLAUDE.md, so the section can outlive the last limit until the next writeRules
   (`write-rules`, `upgrade`, `set-spend-tracking`, `set-profile`, `set-review-mode`, or the next set/unset here).
   JUSTIFICATION: (1) the stale section is instruction text only — spend-guard, the brief and record-gate-review read
   STATE, and with no limit they report no limit and never trip, so nothing misbehaves; (2) adding a rules rewrite to
   archive and remove-epic would put a CLAUDE.md write (a repository file) on two of the engine's most-called write
   paths for a cosmetic benefit, and the unit rung forbids filesystem writes there; (3) it is the same staleness
   `set-spend-tracking`'s section has when state is edited by hand. Asserted: assert/spend-guard.test.mjs "5.3 With
   tracking on the section follows ## Spend tracking, and an archived epic's layer does not count" runs `write-rules`
   after the archive to prove the REFRESHED block drops the section.
   FLAGGED for the orchestrator as a possible follow-up (not a defect against the spec's scenarios, which name only
   set/unset).
