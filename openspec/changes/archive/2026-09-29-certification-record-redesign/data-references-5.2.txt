## THE TRAILER — `Twin-Unchanged: <id> — <reason>`

- Names a functional test id. WRITTEN by the committer, in the commit message only.
- READ by `.githooks/commit-msg` → `drift.mjs --phase commit-msg --message` → `parseTwinExemptions()`
  (certification.mjs:206, over `git interpret-trailers --parse --no-divider`), and by Gate 2's audit
  (`git log --format='%H %(trailers:key=Twin-Unchanged)' BASE..HEAD`, pr-workflow step 6 from 6.6).
- STORED nowhere pm owns: no state.json field, no record file. It lives in the commit object, so it is
  removed only by rewriting the commit (amend before push) — or, after the squash into main, by the
  squash itself, which is why Gate 2 runs on `dev` first (design D4).
- A declared id that is not staged is refused (`not-staged`), so the trailer cannot point at nothing
  at the moment it is accepted.
