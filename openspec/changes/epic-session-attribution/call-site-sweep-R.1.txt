## How each is stamped
- ONE chokepoint: stampTouched() in scripts/lib/store.mjs, called by BOTH stores (disk and memory) inside writeRecord after the no-op early return. Every saveState() above reaches it; none is listed individually because the stamp does not depend on the caller. A save that changes no epic (state.active only, a release, a lesson) stamps nothing: stampTouched compares each epic with its disk pre-image.
- Hook paths (commit-nudge/render self-heal via reconcileArchived under onConflict:"skip"): main() sets no session for a hook verb, so currentSession() is null and nothing is stamped (design D4a).

## Data reference: epic.sessions holds identities (opaque names), not ids, so no dangling-reference sweep is owed.
- writer: stampSession() in store.mjs (one).
- readers: none in the engine; the record and `list --json` expose it.
- REMOVE path: remove-epic deletes the whole epic record and its trail with it (design D5).

## Inverses
- --session has no "unset": it is an input, not a stored setting. epic.sessions has no clear verb: declared set-only in EPIC_FLAGS (APPEND-ONLY history, design D5); a mistyped identity is a true record of what was typed.
