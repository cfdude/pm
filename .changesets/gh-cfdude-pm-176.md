* **`update-epic --story <n>` says it is 1-indexed, and a wrong index can be corrected.** `--help` now renders `--story n, 1-indexed (NOT the 0-indexed stories[] position in state.json)`, and a successful `--story` write echoes the story it landed on. `--story <n> --correct-disposition "<why>"` reopens a story whose `--done` or `--wont-do` was recorded against the wrong index and keeps the original under `stories[n-1].corrections[]`; it may carry `--done` or `--wont-do` to apply the right mutation in the same call. The replacement guard is unchanged and its refusal now names this path. (gh#176)
