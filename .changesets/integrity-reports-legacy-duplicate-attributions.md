* **`integrity` now reports an epic whose `attributedCommits` names one commit more than once (`attributed-commits-hold-duplicates`).** Attribution became once-only in 0.52.0, so a repeated entry was written by an older engine or by hand, and nothing reported it: this repository holds seven such epics, the worst `functional-assertion-test-split` with 58 entries for 35 distinct commits. "The same commit" is decided as every other reader of the array decides it: equal spelling, or a short sha and the full name it abbreviates. The finding names the epic, the entry and distinct-commit counts, and the repeated commits. It prints no remedy on purpose: the gates read the set so nothing is blocked, and no verb removes a single entry from the append-only array (a sha withdrawal removes one occurrence and records a withdrawal the epic never made). It is a read of `state.json` and repairs nothing.
