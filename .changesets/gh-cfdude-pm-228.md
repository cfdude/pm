* **The SessionStart briefing now reports a repo's own rejected lesson `detect:` (#228).** 0.50.0 made the engine refuse a malformed `detect:` with a named reason, but the `lesson-advice` hook must stay silent, so the only surface that reported a reject was pm's own per-commit `lessons-index` test: a consumer repo that wrote a broken `detect:` in its own `docs/lessons/` was told nothing, and its lesson never fired. The SessionStart briefing now carries `⚠ N lesson matcher(s) rejected — the lesson-advice hook never fires it/them; fix the detect: in docs/lessons/:` followed by `- <file>: <reason>` for up to five, and `(+N more in docs/lessons/)` past that. It reads the same `classifyLessons()` the hook projects, prints only when N is above zero, escapes every reason at the print site, and rides on the SessionStart context only: `PROJECT.md` and the PreCompact snapshot (`.conductor/brief.txt`, tracked in some repos) do not depend on `docs/lessons/`. The optional read-only lint verb was not added; the briefing needs no recall.
